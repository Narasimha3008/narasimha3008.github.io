Sep 2026 · Machine Learning

What 1.68M insurance policies taught me about churn

Predicting churn is the easy part. The hard part is making the prediction useful: who do we call first, and what do we say? On 1.68M auto insurance policies, XGBoost did the scoring, but SHAP did the explaining — tenure, recent premium changes, and claim history carried most of the signal.

The lesson I keep coming back to: an interpretable model that the retention team actually trusts beats a black box that scores 2% better. Start with the business question, then pick the model that answers it.

Sep 2026 · SQL

A SQL warehouse that runs 560× faster

I built a star-schema retail warehouse and ran eight analyses on it — cohort retention, RFM, churn. The interesting part wasn't the schema; it was the optimization case study. One missing index turned a multi-minute scan into a sub-second query: roughly 560× faster.

Measure before, measure after. "It feels faster" isn't a result. A timing comparison in the README is what makes an optimization claim real.

Sep 2026 · Dashboards

Building the NYC 311 complaints dashboard

New York's 311 data is messy, huge, and full of real operational questions. I built four views — executive summary, agency performance, geographic intelligence, SLA monitoring — with cross-filtering by borough, year, and status. The SLA breach patterns and resolution-time outliers were the payoff.

One rule guided the layout: one question per view. A dashboard that tries to answer everything answers nothing.

Sep 2026 · Healthcare IT

Epic certification: is it worth it in Wisconsin?

I've been looking into Epic certification because Wisconsin is Epic country — the HQ is in Verona, and Bellin, Froedtert, Advocate Aurora, and UW Health all run on it. For healthcare data work, the analytics-side modules (Cogito, Clarity, Caboodle) are the interesting ones: that's where the reporting and data model live.

The catch I keep hitting: Epic certification usually comes through your employer, with training at their campus. So the realistic path is landing at an Epic shop that certifies you after hire. If you're a healthcare analyst in Wisconsin, it's one of the highest-leverage credentials you can pick up — I'm keeping it on my radar.
